**Date:** 27 Oct 2026 · **Source:** Tejas, a friend of the founder in India, visited the plant. Second-hand notes, not yet checked with the company · **Status:** lead; no direct contact yet · **Analysis:** `Opportunity_Legacy_Factories_India.md`

Company and contact are named with the founder's agreement. This file records the facts and open questions; the analysis, market sizing and value proposition are in the opportunity file.

## 1. Company snapshot

| | |
|---|---|
| Company | Hindustan Pencils Pvt Ltd, Mumbai; founded 1958 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindustan_Pencils)) |
| Brands | Nataraj, Apsara |
| Market position | ~40% of India's pencil market, with DOMS at ~30% (PTI, via [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/pencil-maker-doms-industries-rs-1200-cr-ipo-to-open-on-dec-13)). ~15% of the wider writing-instruments market ([Euromonitor](https://www.euromonitor.com/writing-instruments-in-india/report)) |
| Scale | 8.5M pencils a day across ten factories in five regions ([company site](https://hindustanpencils.com/about-us/), [2018 catalog](https://www.hindustanpencils.com/Hindustan_Pencil_Product_Catalog_2018.pdf)) |
| Revenue | ~₹1,200 crore, per the founder. Not in public filings; the company is private |
| Margin | "Low", as reported. Profit fell from ~₹9 crore (2015) to ~₹1 crore (2018) ([TradeBrains](https://tradebrains.in/?p=52196)). Rival DOMS: 18.2% EBITDA margin in FY25 |
| Staff | ~1,140 company-wide per [Revelio Labs](https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/hindustan-pencils/employees/). That counts online profiles and clearly misses most factory workers |
| Raw material | ~70% of wood slats from Pulwama, Kashmir ([Kashmir Life](https://kashmirlife.net/a-pencil-village-issue-31-vol-12-251040/)) |
| Pollachi plant | Not found in public sources. What it makes (full pencils or parts) is unknown |

## 2. What Tejas reported

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Headcount | 300–1,000 at the plant |
| Machines | 200+, mostly ~20 years old. No reliable way to tell when something is going wrong |
| Staffing | 1 employee per 2–3 machines; 1 supervisor per 3–4 employees |
| Knowledge | Common faults and fixes are undocumented and held by people, many in their 50s |
| Software | SAP only |
| Material movement | ~4 inbound and 2 dispatch vehicles a day. The pain is monitoring |
| Manpower | 15 days of training per new hire; ~30% attrition a year. First relayed as monthly; the founder now believes yearly |
| QC | Pencil checking is a challenge; no detail yet |

The founder notes that the machine staff are probably a different group from the general blue-collar workforce. Keep the two apart when estimating.
